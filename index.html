Security is built in, not bolted on.

Most small business websites are never checked after launch. Ours are. Here is what every VELIX site gets, and what we add for sites that handle customer data.

  1. HTTPS everywhere

    Every page is encrypted from the first visit, with the certificate renewed for you.

  2. Hardened forms

    Contact and quote forms filter spam, bots and injection attempts before they reach you.

  3. Updates and monitoring

    We watch the libraries your site depends on and patch known vulnerabilities quickly.

  4. Automated backups

    If something goes wrong, your site can be restored instead of rebuilt.

  5. Monthly security check

    For stores and platforms handling customer data: an external scan of your public attack surface, with a plain-language report.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Message us and you'll hear back within one business day.

What's actually included in the price?

Every package includes the pages/build scope listed for that tier, a free live design session with unlimited revisions during that session, up to 3 working meetings (max 2 hours each) after that, and an official completion certificate on delivery. Nothing hidden, nothing added later without you agreeing to it first.

How long does a project take?

A focused landing page typically takes 1–2 weeks. A full business website is usually 2–4 weeks. A platform with a dashboard or e-commerce store runs 4–8 weeks. The exact timeline depends on how ready your content is and how many revision rounds we go through.

Do you offer hosting and domains?

Yes — we can handle hosting and domain setup end-to-end, or work with hosting you already have. We'll recommend whichever is fastest and most reliable for your specific site.

Is my site secure?

Every site ships with HTTPS, hardened forms, and dependency monitoring as standard. E-commerce sites and platforms handling customer data get an optional year-long Protection Plan with a real monthly check-up and report.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a post-launch support window. After that, an optional maintenance plan covers updates, backups and small content changes — so you're never left maintaining the site alone unless you want to.

Why choose a newer studio over an established agency?

Because your project gets the founder directly, not a rotating account team. We're upfront that VELIX is newly launched — in exchange, early clients get priority attention and founder-level pricing you won't find once we scale up.
